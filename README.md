# Mshanga - Kenyan Bracelet ChatGPT/Codex Desktop AI Pet

Hi, this is Mark Kaigwa and I'm excited to share Mshanga with you.

Mshanga is a small animated desktop pet inspired by the beaded Kenyan bracelet that makes a Kenyan easy to spot anywhere in the world. We wear them everywhere and it gives us pride to show one special way about who we are and the country we're so proud to be in. I was inspired by our growing use of AI in Kenya, which I've documented in our upcoming “How Kenyans Use AI” study and report (September 2026) by Nendo.

The complete, ready-to-install package is in the [mshanga-pet folder](mshanga-pet/). It includes a transparent 1,536 × 2,288 WebP sprite sheet in the v2 11-row pet layout: nine interaction animations plus 16 attention directions.

## Who can use it

Mshanga works with the pet feature in the **Codex desktop app on a Mac**. If it comes to PC, Linux, or other platforms, then you can use it there, too. It is not a normal image upload for the ChatGPT website or mobile app. You need to see a pet picker in your desktop app before these instructions apply.

## Use it locally

Open [the simple installation guide](mshanga-pet/INSTALL.md), then put the complete `mshanga-pet` folder in your local Codex pet folder and test it in the desktop app.

Full disclosure: I'm not a developer, so you might have bugs or encounter other challenges. I wish I could provide support, but I'm also figuring this out and get busy too. Just letting you know.

## Cultural note

The palette and tactile bead construction were developed from reference photos of Kenyan beaded bracelets supplied for this project. Please keep that context visible when adapting or sharing Mshanga, and avoid presenting it as an official national symbol or as representative of a specific community’s work.

If you want to give credit, you can credit me, Mark Kaigwa or @MKaigwa, across social media, and Nendo Research & Marketing, @Nendo254, across platforms.

## Sharing and experimenting

This repository is intentionally easy to fork. Preserve the atlas dimensions, the spriteVersionNumber: 2 setting, and the transparent background. If you make a remix, credit the bracelet inspiration and explain what you changed. The full licence is [inside the package](mshanga-pet/LICENSE).
