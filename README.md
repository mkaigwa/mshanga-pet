# Mshanga - Kenyan Bracelet ChatGPT/Codex Desktop AI Pet

Hi, this is Mark Kaigwa and I'm excited to share Mshanga with you. 

Mshanga is a small animated desktop pet inspired by the beaded Kenyan bracelet that makes a Kenyan easy to spot anywhere in the world. 
We wear them everywhere and it gives us pride to show one special way about who we are and the country we're so proud to be in.
I was inspired by our growing use of AI in Kenya, which I've documented in our upcoming "How Kenyans Use AI" study and report (September 2026) by Nendo.

The package includes a transparent 1,536 × 2,288 WebP sprite sheet in the v2
11-row pet layout: nine interaction animations plus 16 attention directions.

## Who can use it

Mshanga works with the pet feature in the **Codex desktop app on a Mac**.
If it comes to PC, Linux, or other platforms, then you can use it there, too. 
It is not a normal image upload for the ChatGPT website or mobile app. You
need to see a pet picker in your desktop app before these instructions apply.

## Use it locally

Follow [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) to put this package in your local Codex pet
folder and test it in the desktop app.

Full disclosure: I'm not a developer, so you might have bugs or encounter other challenges, I wish I could provide you support but I'm also figuring things out and get busy, too. Just letting you know.

## What is here

- `spritesheet.webp` — final transparent animated-pet atlas
- `pet.json` — pet metadata
- `docs/standard-animation-sheet.png` — a quick view of the nine core loops
- `docs/look-anchors.png` — its four cardinal attention anchors

## Cultural note

The palette and tactile bead construction were developed from reference photos
of Kenyan beaded bracelets supplied for this project. Please keep that context
visible when adapting or sharing Mshanga, and avoid presenting it as an
official national symbol or as representative of a specific community's work.

If you want to give credit, you can give credit to me (Mark Kaigwa) or @MKaigwa across social media and Nendo Research & Marketing @Nendo254 across platforms.

## Sharing and experimenting

This repository is intentionally easy to fork: preserve the atlas dimensions,
the `spriteVersionNumber: 2` setting, and the transparent background. If you
make a remix, credit the bracelet inspiration and explain what you changed.
