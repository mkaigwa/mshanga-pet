# Contributing

Keep each contribution small and testable.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is shared under the
repository's Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
licence.

- Preserve the 8-column × 11-row v2 atlas layout at 192 × 208 pixels per cell.
- Keep `spritesheet.webp` transparent—do not ship a cyan or pink background.
- Maintain Mshanga's cuff silhouette, woven black/red/white/green beadwork,
  central motif, tiny feet, and one attached bead-hand.
- Make gaze direction with the eyes and inner cuff cues, not by rotating the
  entire bracelet.
- Do not add official crests, government logos, text, or claims of official
  affiliation.

For a visual change, include a before/after image and note which animation or
look directions you retested.
